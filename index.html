How to Play

Movement

Use arrow keys or on-screen buttons to move. You travel as many spaces as the number shown in your target cell.

All traversed cells become walls. Avoid hitting walls or edges!

Scoring

Collect points by traversing cells with higher numbers.

Bonus: Land on a corner (marked with ★) for +25 points!

Keyboard Shortcuts

  • Arrow Keys or WASD - Move
  • U or Z - Undo move

Game Modes

  • Classic - Traditional Greed gameplay
  • Timer - Race against the clock
  • Pre-programmed - Plan moves, watch execution
  • Combined - Timer + Pre-programmed challenge
